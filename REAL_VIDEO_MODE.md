# Real Video Mode V0.8

Production preview is Blender 3D, not the storyboard-card media path.

V0.8 quality contract:
1. Babuji/Guddu/Bittu visible for comedy projects.
2. Renderer-supported physical action and story props.
3. Neural TTS when `edge-tts` is available.
4. TTS edge-silence trim.
5. Adaptive scene duration based on actual audio.
6. No fixed inter-scene pause.
7. Small J-cut only for continuous dialogue.
8. Reaction/punchline holds are intentional and bounded.
9. Final unexplained silence >0.75s fails continuity QA.
10. Lightweight text-driven mouth shapes replace binary puppet mouth motion.
